About this project


    

      

        Garun Mallam Smart Agriculture & Market Hub is a community-focused digital prototype intended to support farmers, agricultural trade, youth services and better delivery information in Garun Mallam, Kano State, Nigeria.
      


      

        The project is being developed as a practical technology portfolio project. Its features will be tested and improved using feedback from local users.
