Making local deliveries easier to organise

In local commerce, incomplete directions can cause delays, wasted journeys and failed deliveries. Garun Mallam Smart Delivery is a prototype concept for organising recipient details, postcode information and useful location instructions in one place.

Who could benefit?

The proposed service focuses on everyday delivery needs in Garun Mallam, Kano State, with potential to expand to other communities.

🌾

Farmers and Produce Sellers

Organise delivery details for agricultural produce and supplies.

🧱

Building-Material Suppliers

Record delivery addresses for cement, rods, tools and other materials.

🚚

Drivers and Local Businesses

Prepare clearer delivery summaries with recipient and location details.

Project Overview

Project: Garun Mallam Smart Delivery

Sector: Delivery, logistics and local commerce

Initial target area: Garun Mallam, Kano State, Nigeria

Current stage: Early working prototype

Proposed next step: Explore integration with the official NIPOST Digital Postcode system, subject to developer access and the relevant API documentation.

Integration status: The official NIPOST Digital Postcode API has not yet been connected. This prototype does not currently verify postcodes or retrieve official address records.

Our Intended Impact

We aim to explore whether clearer delivery information and, if authorised, official building-level postcode integration can help reduce address confusion, improve delivery coordination and support local businesses.

These are intended benefits that will need to be tested with users.
